Privacy notice

Last updated: 29 October 2026

PGFinders is developing an accommodation discovery platform. This launch-stage notice explains the information collected through the early-access form on this website.

What we collect

If you join the early-access list, we collect the email address you submit and basic form metadata (such as the fact that it came from this website). Please do not submit sensitive personal information through this form.

Why we use it

We use your email address to share relevant PGFinders launch and early-access updates. We do not use the form to make accommodation reservations or collect payments.

Where it is processed

This website is configured for Netlify Forms. If deployed with Netlify Forms enabled, the form submission will be processed and stored by Netlify under the site's account. The form is not meant to collect signups in local preview mode.

Retention and your choices

We intend to keep early-access contact details only as long as needed for launch communications or until you ask us to remove them. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact PGFinders through our LinkedIn company page until a dedicated support contact is published.

Changes

We may update this notice as the product and signup process evolve. Before opening signups at scale, PGFinders should review this notice and replace it with a policy appropriate for its actual operations and legal obligations.
