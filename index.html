Good questions.
Clear answers.

We’re building in the open. Here’s what to know about PGFinders right now.

What is PGFinders? +

PGFinders is a technology-enabled accommodation discovery platform being built to help students and young professionals compare PGs and shared stays with greater clarity.

Where will PGFinders launch first? +

We’re starting with Delhi and intend to grow as we learn what residents need from the accommodation search experience.

Can I browse live listings today? +

The product is still being developed. Join the early-access list to hear when the next experience is ready. Please verify a property and its terms directly before making any payment or commitment.

Does joining the list cost anything? +

No. Joining the early-access list is free. It simply lets us contact you with relevant launch updates.
